My Favourate Building

I love Toronto City Hall the most. We wandered around the square right outside the building. I’ve learned that if you look down at it from high above, the whole building looks just like a pair of eyes. It has two curved towers around a round main building, which creates this special eye shape. Even when I stand on the ground, its unusual curved design looks really unique and attractive, so this landmark leaves me a deep impression.

My Favourate Community

My favourite public facility is Toronto’s PATH underground pedestrian system. We walked through part of it on our trip. It links numerous office buildings, shopping malls and subway stations in the downtown financial district. Local residents can walk around indoors without being bothered by rainy or cold weather. There are shops, cafes and rest areas along the passageways, perfectly satisfying people’s daily travel, dining and shopping demands. It is really a convenient public space for the whole community.

Tech In The City

I spotted many practical intelligent devices inside the PATH underground walkway. The automatic fare gates support contactless payment with PRESTO cards and mobile phones; people just need to tap lightly to pass through quickly. Besides, touch-screen digital information kiosks are placed along the corridor. Visitors can look up indoor directions, bus schedules and nearby service information easily on the screens. These smart devices bring great convenience to people’s daily trips in downtown Toronto.
